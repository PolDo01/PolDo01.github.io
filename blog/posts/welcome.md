Hi, I'm Pol. Welcome to my corner of the internet — a place for the things I build, the
tools I use, and the notes I keep along the way.

This is a **placeholder post**, but it's also a working demo: the text you're reading is
plain Markdown, and the whole page was generated from a single file.

## What lives here

- **Projects** — a running record of the software I build and tinker with
- **Notes** — short write-ups when I figure something out the hard way
- **Experiments** — things that may or may not turn into something real

## Why a static site

Static sites are boring in the best way: no server, no database, nothing to break at
3 a.m. A tiny build step turns Markdown into HTML, and GitHub Pages does the hosting.

> If a system can be understood in one evening, it can be maintained for years.

That's the whole philosophy. Keep the pipeline small, keep the writing fast, and keep
the code readable.
